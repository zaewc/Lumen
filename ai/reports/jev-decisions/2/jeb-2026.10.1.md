## Results

With the evidence guards
([ADR-0043](../../../../docs/decisions/0043-discard-removable-answers-the-evidence-does-not-support.md)),
next to prompt v1 on the same cases ([report](../1/jeb-2026.10.1.md)):

| Set | Prompt | Critical FP (answered) | 95% upper bound | Precision | Recall | Abstention | Canary flips |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Golden | v1 | 71 / 1,103 | 8.05% | 59.7% | 96.9% | 7.2% | |
| Golden | v2 | **14 / 1,103** | **2.12%** | 68.6% | 83.3% | 24.8% | |
| Adversarial | v1 | 6 / 160 | 7.98% | 33.3% | 18.8% | 33.0% | 17 |
| Adversarial | v2 | 1 / 160 | 3.43% | 37.5% | 18.8% | 49.4% | **20** |

Before the guards: v2 golden 16 / 1,103 (v1 81), adversarial 5 / 160 (v1 23), and
**32 canary flips** (v1 19). The guards remove 12 of v2's flips.

Per split (golden, with the guards), the critical false positives of v1 → v2 are
15 → 2 of 228 (`dev`), 14 → 4 of 232 (`calibration`) and 42 → 8 of 643
(`test-locked`); the paired difference is significant on every split (McNemar
p < 0.001).

**Release gate (full): fails** (target 0.50%). Jeb stays demote-only for every class
([ADR-0020](../../../../docs/decisions/0020-jev-evidence-only-judge.md)).

## What it means for the app

The app asks the Decisions questions (v1 or v2) only about items the policy proposes
for quarantine, and Jeb can only demote them to review (ADR-0020). Review items are
never assessed: they get the separate `jev-describe/1` question, which asks only what
kind of item it is (ADR-0041). Paired on the same golden cases, with the guards:

| Items | Measure | v1 | v2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| With positive evidence (a knowledge-base entry or a caches location: what quarantine proposals carry) | critical judged removable, confident | 5 / 44 | 6 / 44 |
| | critical judged removable, any confidence | 14 / 44 | 19 / 44 |
| | removable found | 116 / 117 | 117 / 117 |
| Without positive evidence (not asked today) | critical judged removable, confident | 66 / 1,059 | **8 / 1,059** |
| | critical judged removable, any confidence | 258 / 1,059 | 105 / 1,059 |
| | removable found | 291 / 303 | 233 / 303 |

- On what the app asks today, v2 is no better: it lets slightly more of the 44 needed
  items with positive evidence through as removable (the sample is small).
- v2's gain is on items without positive evidence, which only matters if Jeb is ever
  asked about them, for example to let AI influence review items.
- v2 follows injected instructions more often before the guards (32 flips against 19);
  after them, 20 against 17. Of the 20, 6 are in `local_model_store` (Ollama,
  LM Studio), 5 in `app_support_data`, 3 in `developer_state` (Xcode archives) and 3
  in `user_content`.

Production stays on v1 (roadmap 14.4).
