# Prompt v2 on the dev split of 2026.10.1

Recorded 2026-10-26 with `lumen-jev-eval record --split dev --prompt v2` (361 requests,
361 answered), with the owner's approval. Cassette:
`ai/cassettes/jev-decisions/2/jeb-2026.10.1-dev.json`; guarded report:
`jeb-2026.10.1-dev.json` (`lumen-jev-eval run --split dev --prompt v2 --json`).
Compared with prompt v1's answers to the same 361 requests (from the full 2026.10.1
cassette) by `ai/tools/compare_cassettes.py`.

## Summary

- Critical false positives on the dev split fall from 17 to **2** of 228 without the
  guards (15 to 2 with them); the 95% upper bound from 11.67% to **3.13%**. The paired
  difference is significant (McNemar p < 0.001).
- Recall falls from 96.2% to 80.0% and abstention rises to 23%: v2 answers `uncertain`
  where it lacks positive evidence, as it was told to.
- What the app actually asks about is proposed items, which always carry positive
  evidence (a knowledge-base entry or a caches location). On removable cases with
  positive evidence recall stays at **100%** (47/47) for both prompts, so v2 would not
  demote more real proposals to review. The removable cases v2 now leaves `uncertain`
  are those without positive evidence (recall 93.1% → 63.8%).
- Caveat: among the 6 critical cases that do carry positive evidence, v2 answered 3
  `likely_removable` (any confidence) against 0 for v1; the sample is too small to
  judge, and the full recording should check it.
- Canary flips: 0 for both on this split.

Next (roadmap 14.4): record v2 on the calibration and test-locked splits (about 1,400
requests, the owner's approval needed) before switching production to v2.
