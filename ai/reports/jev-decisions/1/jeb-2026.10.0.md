## The critical false positive

`macos.developer.archives.0001`, an Xcode archive
(`~/Library/Developer/Xcode/Archives/…/Example 1.4.xcarchive`). It holds the symbols
for a shipped build and cannot be regenerated; the evidence says
`regeneratable: false`. Jeb answered `likely_removable` (probability 0.74, confidence
0.61), citing a knowledge-base match.

This answer cannot change what Lumen does:

- Jeb is asked only about items the policy already proposes for quarantine
  ([ADR-0036](../../../../docs/decisions/0036-jeb-on-the-school-ai-hub.md)). The
  knowledge base has no entry for Xcode archives, and the policy never proposes
  unmatched data for quarantine, so they are kept and never sent.
- Its answers are evidence that can only make a decision more cautious
  ([ADR-0020](../../../../docs/decisions/0020-jev-evidence-only-judge.md)).

So Jeb stays demote-only for every class, as the evaluation rules require.
