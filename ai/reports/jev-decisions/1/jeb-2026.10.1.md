## Results

| Set | Unanswered | Critical FP (answered) | 95% upper bound | Precision | Recall | Abstention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Golden | 0 | 81 / 1,103 (7.3%) | 9.05% | 54.4% | 96.9% | 3.1% |
| Adversarial | 0 | 23 / 160 | 20.78% | 21.7% | 81.2% | 4.0% |

**Release gate (full): fails**, by a wide margin (target 0.50%). Jeb stays demote-only
for every class
([ADR-0020](../../../../docs/decisions/0020-jev-evidence-only-judge.md)).

## Where it fails

| Stratum | Cases | Critical FP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `developer_state` (Xcode archives and user data, IDE settings) | 96 | 30 |
| `app_support_data` (TextEdit autosave, app databases) | 285 | 17 |
| `user_content` (Sketch autosave, projects) | 80 | 12 |
| `security_module` (e.g. Malwarebytes quarantine) | 190 | 9 |
| `unknown_executable` | 60 | 7 |
| `license_data` (Adobe PCD, FLEXnet) | 45 | 4 |
| `installer_cache` (Windows Package Cache) | 10 | 2 |

- The false positives are mostly confident-looking (`likely_removable` with probability
  0.7–0.8), usually citing `knowledge_base_match` or `stale_by_time`, often against
  `regeneratable: false` evidence.
- Strong strata: game saves, media libraries, Docker volumes, local model stores and
  every removable stratum (recall 96.9%).
- **Canary flips:** 19, spread over the payload families (a few each). Jeb's answers are
  not deterministic, so part of this is noise between two runs of near-identical
  requests rather than the payload; either way it is not resistant enough to be
  trusted with removal.

## What it means for the app

Nothing changes in what Lumen does: Jeb is asked only about items the policy already
proposes for quarantine, and an answer can only make a decision more cautious; no code
path lets a judge relax one. These results are the evidence that it must stay that way
for Jeb.
