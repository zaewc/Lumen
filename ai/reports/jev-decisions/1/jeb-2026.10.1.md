## Results

With the evidence guards
([ADR-0043](../../../../docs/decisions/0043-discard-removable-answers-the-evidence-does-not-support.md)),
which discard removable answers the request's own evidence does not support; the
cassette is unchanged:

| Set | Unanswered | Critical FP (answered) | 95% upper bound | Precision | Recall | Abstention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Golden | 0 | 71 / 1,103 (6.4%) | 8.05% | 59.7% | 96.9% | 7.2% |
| Adversarial | 0 | 6 / 160 | 7.98% | 33.3% | 18.8% | 33.0% |

Before the guards: golden 81 / 1,103 (bound 9.05%; precision 54.4%, recall 96.9%,
abstention 3.1%), adversarial 23 / 160 (bound 20.78%; recall 81.2%), 19 canary flips.
Adversarial recall fell because the 16 reverse-injection canaries now come back
`uncertain`, which their labels accept. The analysis is in
[research 17](../../../../docs/research/17-jeb-error-analysis.md).

**Release gate (full): fails**, by a wide margin (target 0.50%). Jeb stays demote-only
for every class
([ADR-0020](../../../../docs/decisions/0020-jev-evidence-only-judge.md)).

## Where it fails

| Stratum | Cases | Critical FP (before the guards) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `developer_state` (Xcode archives and user data, IDE settings) | 96 | 25 (30) |
| `app_support_data` (TextEdit autosave, app databases) | 285 | 16 (17) |
| `user_content` (Sketch autosave, projects) | 80 | 10 (12) |
| `security_module` (e.g. Malwarebytes quarantine) | 190 | 9 (9) |
| `unknown_executable` | 60 | 5 (7) |
| `license_data` (Adobe PCD, FLEXnet) | 45 | 4 (4) |
| `installer_cache` (Windows Package Cache) | 10 | 2 (2) |

- The false positives are confident-looking (`likely_removable` with probability
  0.70–0.89) and cite a plausible reason: `knowledge_base_match` without any
  knowledge-base entry (Xcode archives and dSYMs), `regeneratable_cache` on data with
  "cache" or "tmp" in its name, or `stale_by_time`. The guards removed those that
  ignored `regeneratable: false` or gave no reason for removal.
- Strong strata: game saves, media libraries, Docker volumes, local model stores and
  every removable stratum (recall 96.9%).
- **Canary flips:** 17 (19 before the guards), spread over the payload families (a few
  each); every remaining one has a twin answered `likely_needed`. Jeb's answers are
  not deterministic, so part of this is noise between two runs of near-identical
  requests rather than the payload; either way it is not resistant enough to be
  trusted with removal.

## What it means for the app

Nothing changes in what Lumen does: Jeb is asked only about items the policy already
proposes for quarantine, and an answer can only make a decision more cautious; no code
path lets a judge relax one. These results are the evidence that it must stay that way
for Jeb.
